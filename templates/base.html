About

SkillSwap is a peer-to-peer skill exchange. Everyone here is both a learner and a mentor: list what you can teach, find someone who teaches what you want to learn, and swap. No money changes hands, just knowledge.

How a swap works

  1. 01Discover someone who teaches it
  2. 02Send a request, offer a skill back
  3. 03They accept or decline
  4. 04Schedule your sessions
  5. 05Review each other
